# Competitor Analysis — Family Media Guide Websites

**Date:** February 21, 2026
**Sites analyzed:** Common Sense Media, Allocine, Filmages.ch

---

## Common Sense Media (commonsensemedia.org)

The most relevant competitor — US-based, non-profit, family-focused media guide.

### What they do best

| Pattern | Details | Relevance for us |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Age badge as hero metric** | Green shield "age 6+" is the primary visual identifier on every card | Our age badge exists but could be more prominent |
| **Three-perspective reviews** | Expert + Parent + Kid reviews create layered trust | We have expert + community — consider separating parent/kid |
| **Content breakdown grid** | Violence, Language, Sex, etc. with severity levels | We have this (contentMetrics) — already a strength |
| **Positive content sections** | Role Models, Educational Value, Diversity | We track these (positiveMessages, roleModels) — show them more |
| **Discussion prompts** | "Talk to Your Kids About" section on detail pages | Unique value-add we could replicate |
| **Free core value** | Age ratings accessible without signup | Our model matches this — good |

### Navigation structure

- **Primary:** Movies, TV, Books, Apps, Games, Parenting (6 items)
- **Dual search:** In sticky header AND hero section
- **Age browsing:** Prominent in navigation (Preschoolers 2-4, through Teens 13-15)
- **Breadcrumbs** throughout
- **Curated lists:** "50 Modern Movies All Kids Should Watch Before They're 12"

### Card design

- Poster (2:3 ratio) + age shield badge + one-line editorial summary
- Horizontal carousels on homepage, grids on browse pages
- Age badge is the most scannable element (not the poster)

### Trust signals

- "Since 2003" — longevity
- 501(c)(3) nonprofit badge
- Partner logos: Apple TV+, Netflix, Target
- Review counts (100s per film from parents and kids)

## Allocine (allocine.fr)

The French reference for cinema — entertainment-focused, not family-focused.

### What they do best

| Pattern | Details | Relevance for us |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Strong accent color** | Yellow #FECC00 across all interactive elements | Our violet gradient is our equivalent — be consistent |
| **Dual rating display** | Press (critics) + Spectateurs (audience) stars | We have expert + community — similar concept |
| **Streaming buttons** | Branded platform buttons (Netflix, Disney+, etc.) | We already have streaming data — show where to watch |
| **Engagement loop** | Want to see → Rate → Collect → Share | Our favorites/watchlist could follow this pattern |
| **Always-on search** | Search in sticky header, always accessible | We have this |

### Card design

- Poster (310x420, ~2:3) + star ratings (press + audience) + status labels
- Horizontal carousels + grids
- No age information (not family-focused)

### Navigation

- Films, Séries TV, Box Office, Vidéos, Horaires, Actualité
- Sticky header with prominent search
- Extensive streaming platform integration

### Note

Allocine is ad-supported with deep ad integration. Not relevant to our model. Their streaming marketplace (branded buttons answering "where to watch") is worth adopting.

## Filmages.ch (filmages.ch)

Swiss French cantonal cinema commission — governmental, institutional.

### What they teach (mostly by contrast)

| Pattern | Details | Takeaway |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Dual age system** | "Âge légal" (legal minimum) + "Âge suggéré" (recommended) | Interesting concept — the gap between legal and appropriate is meaningful. We already have official classification + expert recommendation |
| **Glossary tooltips** | Every rating criterion has a tooltip definition | Good transparency pattern — explain what "Violence niveau 3" means |
| **Detailed synthesis** | Paragraph explaining *why* ratings were chosen | Valuable but we already have this via AI-generated reviews |
| **Plain table listings** | No images, no cards, no visual design | What NOT to do |
| **No responsive design** | Fixed-width, jQuery 1.4 | What NOT to do |

### Trust signals

- Official government commission logos
- Since 1999
- 12,716 films catalogued

## Key Takeaways for Le Bon Avis Numérique

### Already strong (keep doing)

1. Content metrics breakdown (violence, language, etc.) — matches CSM's approach
2. Positive content tracking (educational, role models) — rare feature
3. Age-based browsing system — core differentiator
4. Free core value without paywall — matches CSM model
5. Streaming platform data — can match Allocine's "where to watch"

### Biggest gaps vs competitors

1. **Age browsing not in header** — CSM puts it front and center
2. **No one-line editorial summary on cards** — CSM cards have a tagline, we don't
3. **No "Talk to Your Kids About" prompts** — unique CSM feature worth adopting
4. **Streaming availability not shown on cards** — Allocine's branded buttons are effective
5. **No breadcrumb navigation** — CSM uses breadcrumbs throughout
6. **Brand identity confusion** — Neither competitor has this problem

### Design patterns worth adopting (prioritized)

1. **Make age badge THE hero element on cards** — larger, more scannable (CSM)
2. **Add age browsing to header navigation** — primary entry point (CSM)
3. **Show streaming availability on detail pages** with branded platform icons (Allocine)
4. **Add editorial one-liner to cards** — e.g., "Aventure émouvante pour toute la famille, quelques scènes tendues" (CSM)
5. **Add "En parler avec vos enfants" section** on detail pages (CSM)
6. **Use consistent accent color** for all interactive elements — our violet (Allocine's approach)
7. **Show both official classification and expert recommendation** with clear labels (Filmages dual system — we already have this)
