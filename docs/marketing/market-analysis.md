## 2. Analytics — Plausible Recommended

**Why Plausible over GA4:**
- CNIL-exempt (no consent banner needed) — critical in France where 40-60% refuse cookies
- EU-hosted (Germany), no US data transfer
- Cookie-free, privacy-first
- $9/month for 10k pageviews (or self-host free)
- Clean single-dashboard vs GA4 complexity

Vercel Analytics as supplement for Web Vitals only.

## 3. Social Scheduling

**Keep Resend for transactional**, add Brevo for newsletter:

## 6. Day-1 Quick Wins

### Directories (all free)
- Google Business Profile
- Pages Jaunes / Solocal
- WebRankInfo (French SEO community + directory)
- BetaList, Crunchbase, F6S

### Backlink Strategies
- Guest posts on MagicMaman, Doctissimo
- Create shareable infographics (PEGI ratings guide, screen time by age)
- Build a free quiz tool: "Quel film pour quel age?"
- Contact CLEMI for institutional .gouv backlink
- HARO / Connectively: respond to journalist queries

### Structured Data (biggest SEO win)
Priority order:
1. **Review + AggregateRating** on every media page — stars in Google results
2. **FAQPage** on guide pages — expandable snippets
3. **Movie/VideoGame/Book/TVSeries** entity markup
4. **BreadcrumbList** site-wide

## 8. Killer SEO Template

For every major film/show/game, create a page:
- URL: `/media/[titre]`
- Title: "[Titre] — A partir de quel age? Avis parents | Totem Avisé"
- Target keyword: "[titre] a partir de quel age"

This captures the highest-intent French parent searches.
