# Le Bon Avis Numerique — Marketing & Growth Playbook

## Identity

**Brand name:** Le Bon Avis Numerique
**Tagline:** Le guide independant des familles pour des choix medias eclaires
**Domain:** lebonsens-numerique.fr (to be confirmed)
**Tone:** Expert but warm. Trustworthy parent speaking to other parents — never preachy, never corporate.

## Target Audience

### Primary: French Parents (30-45 ans)
- Children aged 3-15
- Tech-literate but overwhelmed by choice
- Care about screen time quality, not just quantity
- Trust expert reviews over user ratings
- Search in French: "film pour enfant 7 ans", "jeu video adapte 10 ans"

### Secondary: Educators & Librarians
- Looking for curated lists by age
- Value the content metrics (violence, language, positive messages)
- Share recommendations with parents

### Tertiary: Teens (13-17)
- Looking for what's new and age-appropriate
- Discover through TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
- Care about streaming availability

## Competitive Positioning

### What we are NOT:
- Not AlloCine (general public, no age guidance)
- Not Common Sense Media (English-only, US-centric)
- Not PEGI/CSA (just a number, no nuance)

### What we ARE:
- The French Common Sense Media — expert reviews WITH detailed content breakdowns
- Family-first: every piece of content rated on 7 dimensions (violence, language, sex/nudity, substance use, positive messages, role models, educational value)
- Multi-media: films, series, jeux video, livres in one place
- Age-specific: browse by exact age range, not just "PG-13"

### Unique Value Proposition (one sentence):
"Le seul guide francais qui analyse chaque film, serie et jeu video sur 7 criteres pour vous aider a choisir en confiance."

## Voice & Messaging

### Do:
- Use "vous" (formal but warm)
- Emphasize independence: "avis independants", "sans publicite"
- Speak to real parent concerns: "est-ce que c'est trop violent?", "mon fils de 8 ans peut-il regarder ca?"
- Use concrete examples: "Violence 2/5 : quelques scenes de bagarre comique"
- Celebrate good content: "Un chef-d'oeuvre familial" > "Pas de contenu inapproprie"

### Don't:
- Never shame parents for screen time
- Never sound like a government agency
- Never use English when French exists
- Never use exclamation marks in CTAs (calm authority > hype)
- Never promise "safe" — promise "informed choices"

### Key Messages by Channel:

**Homepage/SEO:**
"Trouvez le film, la serie ou le jeu parfait pour votre enfant. Avis d'experts, recommandations par age, contenus analyses en detail."

**Social Media:**
"[Film] sort cette semaine. Adapte a partir de quel age? Notre analyse complete >"
"Top 5 des jeux cooperatifs pour jouer en famille ce week-end"

**Email:**
"Cette semaine sur Le Bon Avis : 12 nouveaux films analyses, notre selection vacances, et le guide 'Premiers jeux video'"

## SEO Strategy

### Priority Keywords (search volume FR):
1. "film pour enfant [age]" — e.g., "film pour enfant 6 ans"
2. "jeu video enfant [age]"
3. "avis [film name] enfant" — e.g., "avis vaiana 2 enfant"
4. "serie netflix enfant"
5. "film famille"
6. "a partir de quel age [film]"
7. "contenu violent [film/jeu]"
8. "meilleur film animation [annee]"

### Content Strategy:
- Every media detail page = long-tail SEO page ("vaiana-2-avis-age-recommande")
- Age range pages = category SEO ("/age/5-7" targets "film enfant 5 ans")
- Collection pages = thematic SEO ("/collections/disney-classics")
- Blog (when launched): "Guide: premiers jeux video pour votre enfant" type articles

### Technical SEO:
- sitemap.ts auto-generates from DB media items
- robots.ts allows all except /admin and /api
- Structured data (JSON-LD) for Review schema on media pages
- OG images per media item (poster + age badge)

## Social Media Strategy

### Instagram (@lebonavisnumerique)
- **Content mix:** 40% new release reviews, 30% thematic lists, 20% parenting tips, 10% behind-the-scenes
- **Format:** Carousel posts (slide 1: poster + age badge, slide 2-4: content breakdown, slide 5: verdict)
- **Stories:** "Cette semaine au cinema" weekly feature, polls ("A quel age avez-vous montre Star Wars?")
- **Reels:** 15-30s "En 30 secondes" quick review format
- **Frequency:** 3-4 posts/week, daily stories

### TikTok (@lebonavisnumerique)
- **Content:** "A partir de quel age?" format — show poster, reveal age + why
- **Trends:** React to new releases, "Unpopular opinions" on kids media
- **Frequency:** 2-3 videos/week

### Facebook (Le Bon Avis Numerique)
- **Audience:** The actual decision-making parents (35-50)
- **Content:** Full reviews shared from site, "Ce week-end on regarde..." recommendations
- **Groups:** Join/create French parenting groups, be helpful not spammy
- **Frequency:** 3-4 posts/week

### Twitter/X (@BonAvisNum)
- **Content:** Quick takes on new releases, respond to trending entertainment discussions
- **Frequency:** Daily

## Launch Marketing Plan

### Phase 0: Pre-launch (2 weeks before)
- [ ] Create all social media accounts (reserve handles)
- [ ] Set up professional email (contact@, presse@)
- [ ] Prepare 10 "launch day" social posts
- [ ] Write press release (for French tech/parenting press)
- [ ] Create "coming soon" teaser posts
- [ ] Seed 20+ complete media reviews (flagship content)

### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Week 1)
- [ ] Share with personal network (friends, family, parent groups)
- [ ] Post on French Reddit (r/france, r/parentalite if exists)
- [ ] Post on French parenting forums (MagicMaman, Doctissimo, Mumsnet FR)
- [ ] Submit to Product Hunt (French product angle)
- [ ] Email 10 parenting bloggers/influencers with personalized pitch

### Phase 2: Content Marketing (Weeks 2-4)
- [ ] Publish 2 guide articles: "Comment choisir un jeu video pour votre enfant" + seasonal guide
- [ ] Start Instagram/TikTok posting cadence
- [ ] Create shareable infographics: "Guide des ages" poster
- [ ] Pitch to French tech press: Numerama, FrAndroid, Journal du Geek (media family angle)

### Phase 3: Community Building (Month 2+)
- [ ] Launch newsletter (bi-weekly: new reviews + recommendations)
- [ ] Invite first 100 users to review (build UGC)
- [ ] Partner with family-focused streamers/YouTubers
- [ ] Engage with French school associations (FCPE, PEEP)
- [ ] Attend/sponsor family-oriented events (salons du jeu video, festivals du film)

### Phase 4: Growth (Month 3+)
- [ ] SEO content: target "a partir de quel age [film]" for every new release
- [ ] Google Ads: bid on "[film name] avis enfant" for new releases
- [ ] Referral program: "Partagez avec un autre parent"
- [ ] Partnerships with streaming platforms (Netflix Family, Disney+)
